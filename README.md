## A little about me

I'm a **Cognitive Science student at UC Berkeley** interested in the intersection of **AI, software engineering, and human behavior**.

I started out wondering how intelligent systems work and gradually became more interested in the bigger question: **how do we build technology that actually makes sense for the people using it?**

I enjoy working across the stack, from building AI and ML systems to designing the interfaces around them. I'm especially drawn to LLM applications, retrieval, computer vision, intelligent products, and human computer interaction.

I'm still learning a lot, and honestly, that's one of my favorite parts.

> **Talent might open the door. Curiosity makes you walk through it, and hard work keeps you moving.**
