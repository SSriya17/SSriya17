## A little about me

I'm a **Cognitive Science student at UC Berkeley** interested in the intersection of **AI, software engineering, and human behavior**.

I started out curious about how intelligent systems work, but that curiosity grew into a bigger interest in how people adapt to the technology they create. That's what drew me to Cognitive Science: I want to understand how people think, learn, and adapt while exploring how technology can extend what we're capable of.

I enjoy working across the stack, from building AI and ML systems to designing the interfaces around them. I'm especially interested in LLM applications, retrieval, computer vision, intelligent products, and human computer interaction.

I'm still figuring out where all of that will take me, but I think that's the fun part.

> **Talent might open the door. Curiosity makes you walk through it, and hard work keeps you moving.**
