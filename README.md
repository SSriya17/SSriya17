## A little about me

I'm a **Cognitive Science student at UC Berkeley** interested in the intersection of **AI, software engineering, and human behavior**.

What initially drew me to technology was the question of how people adapt to the things we create. That curiosity eventually led me to Cognitive Science, where I can explore both sides of that relationship: **how people think, learn, and adapt, and how technology can extend what we're capable of.**

I enjoy building across the stack, especially when there's an interesting problem behind what I'm making. Lately, that has meant working with **AI and ML systems, LLM applications, retrieval, computer vision, intelligent products, and human computer interaction.**

I'm still figuring out where all of that will take me, but I think that's the fun part.

> *Talent might open the door. Curiosity makes you walk through it, and hard work keeps you moving.*
